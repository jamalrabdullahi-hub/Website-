The State-Backed Guardian of Somali Natural Wealth

The Primary Resources Corporation of Somalia, commonly known as PriRecos, is the official sovereign enterprise charged with managing, surveying, and licensing the vast natural assets of the Federal Republic of Somalia. Operating under national developmental frameworks, the Corporation acts as the central gateway for international joint ventures, exploration leases, and strategic concession partnerships.

Our mission at PriRecos is to unlock the unmatched economic potential of Somalia's primary resources in a sustainable, transparent, and highly secure manner. By utilizing modern surveying methodologies and geological data compilation, we provide international operators and institutional investors with priority access to high-yield blocks across mining, agriculture, fisheries, and hydrocarbon deposits.

Strategic Operational Sectors & Concessions

1. Hydrocarbons & Offshore Energy Blocks

Somalia possesses some of the world's most promising unexplored offshore basins. PriRecos coordinates with global energy firms to facilitate seismic surveying, oil block leasing, and pipeline infrastructure planning. The Primary Resources Corporation of Somalia is dedicated to ensuring that hydrocarbon development complies with top-tier international environmental guidelines.

2. Mining, Rare Earth Elements, & Mineral Exploration

The inland geology of Somalia is highly rich in gold, copper, iron ore, gypsum, and valuable rare earth elements. PriRecos establishes robust licensing frameworks for mineral exploration, allowing certified operators to secure concessions and invest in long-term processing facilities that stimulate regional employment.

3. Blue Economy, Marine Resources, & Sustainable Fisheries

Boasting the longest coastline on mainland Africa, Somalia's marine exclusive economic zone (EEZ) offers world-class commercial fishing and maritime trade routes. We regulate commercial licensing allocations, ensuring sustainable harvests and safeguarding the marine ecology while driving growth in processed seafood exports.

4. High-Yield Agriculture & Arable Land Development

With fertile river basins and vast livestock reserves, Somalia is poised to expand its position as an agricultural powerhouse in East Africa. Through PriRecos, international agricultural consortia can partner on modern irrigation projects, food security partnerships, and livestock export hubs.

Commitment to Transparency, Security, and Growth

At the core of the Primary Resources Corporation of Somalia's mandate is absolute adherence to international compliance standards. Every single concession or exploration permit managed by PriRecos is logged transparently, guaranteeing legal certainty and commercial protection for our investment partners. By bridge-building between public policy and private capital, we aim to forge a prosperous, industrial-grade future for the Horn of Africa.
