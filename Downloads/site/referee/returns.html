Celinta iyo cabashada

Qabyo — qareen ayaa eegaya. Sebtembar 2026.

Muddada

7 maalmood markaad alaabta qaadato ayaad cabasho furi kartaa — bogga “Dalabyadayda”, badhanka Cabasho.

Waxa la aqbalayo

  • Alaabtu ma ahan tii la sheegay (nooc, cabbir, midab).
  • Way jabtay ama way khaldan tahay markii la keenay.
  • Qayb ayaa ka maqan.
  • Alaab been-abuur ah oo loo iibiyay mid asal ah.

Waxa aan la aqbalayn

  • “Way iga beddeshay” kadib markaad isticmaashay.
  • Waxyeello adiga kaa timid.
  • Alaab la dalbaday si gaar ah (custom) oo sax ah.

Sidee u shaqeeyaa

  1. Fur cabasho, ku qor waxa dhacay oo sawir soo dir haddii suurtogal tahay.
  2. Garsoore ayaa eegaya diiwaanka, sawirada bakhaarka iyo miisaanka.
  3. Go'aan 48 saac gudahood: lacag celin buuxda, lacag celin qayb ah, ama diidmo leh sabab cad.

Lacag celinta

Lacagta waxaa lagu celiyaa isla habkii aad ku bixisay (EVC Plus, ZAAD, Sahal, Premier) 24 saac gudahood go'aanka kadib.

Returns and complaints

Draft — under legal review. September 2026.

The window

You have 7 days from collecting the goods to open a complaint — on “My orders”, the Complaint button.

What we accept

  • The goods are not what was described (type, size, colour).
  • They arrived broken or faulty.
  • A part is missing.
  • A counterfeit sold as genuine.

What we do not accept

  • “I changed my mind” after use.
  • Damage you caused.
  • Correctly supplied custom orders.

How it works

  1. Open a complaint, describe what happened, send a photo if you can.
  2. Garsoore checks the record, the warehouse photos and the weight.
  3. A decision within 48 hours: full refund, partial refund, or a refusal with a clear reason.

Refunds

Money goes back the same way you paid (EVC Plus, ZAAD, Sahal, Premier) within 24 hours of the decision.
