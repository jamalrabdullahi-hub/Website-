Help

Questions? WhatsApp: +252 77 242 8472 · Km4 counter, Mogadishu.

How do I buy?

Search for the item or paste a Chinese link. Press “Buy now”, choose where to collect it, pay with EVC / ZAAD / Sahal / Premier, then enter the transaction number. We check it, and then you can follow the order.

When do I get it?

Local goods: today. From China: ~20 days by air or ~38 by sea. Your order page shows the expected date.

What is the pickup code?

Six digits shown only to you when the goods are ready. Show it at the Km4 counter once you have checked the item. The seller is not paid until the code is entered — that is how your money is protected.

What if the item is wrong?

Open a complaint within 7 days — see Returns.

Do I need an account?

You can browse without one. An account (number + PIN) is needed to buy, so you can follow your order.

Do you have a business?

See Business: wholesale buying, FBG (buy in China, we receive, store and sell it for you), and agents who sell your goods for you.
