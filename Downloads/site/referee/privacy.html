Qabyo — qareen ayaa eegaya. Sebtembar 2026.

Waxa aan kaydinno

  • Magacaaga iyo lambarka taleefankaaga (waa akoonkaaga).
  • Cinwaanka gaarsiinta iyo lambarka lacag bixinta, haddii aad kaydiso.
  • Dalabyadaada, codsiyada qiimaha iyo faallooyinkaaga.
  • Tirakoob aan magac lahayn (bogagga la eegay) si aan u hagaajinno bogga.

Waxa aynaan kaydin

PIN-kaaga si toos ah looma kaydiyo — waxaa la kaydiyaa qaab la shiiday (hash). Kaarka bangiga ma qaadanno. Ilaha kale wax xog ah kaagama iibsanno.

Cidda aan la wadaagno

Iibiyaha gudaha ama wakiilka waxaa la siiyaa oo keliya waxa loo baahan yahay si dalabku u dhammaado. Xogtaada lama iibiyo.

Xuquuqdaada

Waad codsan kartaa in xogtaada lagu tuso ama la tirtiro — nala soo xidhiidh. Dalabyada la bixiyay waa in la hayo xisaabaad ahaan.

Privacy

Draft — under legal review. September 2026.

What we keep

  • Your name and phone number (that is your account).
  • Your delivery address and payment number, if you save them.
  • Your orders, price requests and reviews.
  • Anonymous counts of pages viewed, so we can improve the site.

What we do not keep

Your PIN is never stored as typed — only a hash of it. We take no bank cards. We buy no data about you from anyone.

Who we share with

A local seller or an agent is given only what is needed to complete the order. Your data is not sold.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your data — contact us. Paid orders must be kept for accounting.
